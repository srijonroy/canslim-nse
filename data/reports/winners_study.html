Big-winner study: what came before NSE stocks doubled

For every stock and every week: did the price at least double within the next 12 months? Each signal is measured on 2016-2021, then checked on 2023-2025 (2022 is left out so the two don't overlap). Universe: price ≥ Rs 10, ≥ Rs 0.5 Cr traded a day, 1 year of history, earnings data. Green = at least 25% above the base rate; red = at least 20% below.

Caveat: price data covers only stocks still listed today. Companies that collapsed and delisted are missing, so every number here is flattered; compare rows with each other, not with zero.

  • Price strength is the most reliable sign. Stocks already up 100%+ in six months doubled again about twice as often as average, in both periods.
  • Earnings help, but only two signals held up in both periods: operating margin up 6+ points, and profit up 50%+ in each of the last two quarters. These drive the earnings monitor in the daily report.
  • Fast sales growth and long growth streaks were a trap in 2016-21 (negative median return, more crashes) and only worked in 2023-25.
  • "Doubling" partly just measures volatility: tiny and very volatile stocks double more often, and crash more often too.

Can a model learn to spot doublers?

A gradient-boosting model (scikit-learn) was trained on 2016-21 only, then scored on 2023-25. Its weekly top-20 picks doubled 49.5% of the time on the years it learned from, but only 21.6% on the unseen years (base rate 14.2%), about the same as the CAN SLIM filters, and its favourites had the highest crash rate. It is not used by the daily scan.

What it leaned on most (drop in accuracy when that input is shuffled):

  • Volatility (3-month) (0.029)
  • 6-month return (0.017)
  • Size (traded value rank) (0.015)
  • Industry group strength (0.006)
  • 6-month momentum (skip last month) (0.004)
  • Up-day vs down-day volume (0.004)
  • Operating margin change vs a year ago (pts) (0.003)
  • Distance above 50-day average (0.003)
