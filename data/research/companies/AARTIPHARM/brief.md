## The business in 5 lines
- Aarti Pharmalabs (Aarti group demerger, 2022) makes xanthine derivatives (caffeine, theophylline; ~50% of Q2 FY27 sales), generic APIs/intermediates (30%) and CDMO/CMO for innovator pharma (13%). Source: docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.3.
- Xanthine is the cash cow: capacity raised from ~5,000-6,000 to 9,600 tonnes a year (Nov call; the annual report says 9,000). Management calls it the world's second/third largest, with 80-90% of competition in China. docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.4.
- CDMO is the growth pillar: 23 customers, 59 projects (39 commercial, 20 in development). Target Rs 1,000 crore CDMO revenue ("whichever comes first" vs US$100 million). docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.9.
- Size: TTM sales Rs 2,161 crore, operating profit Rs 487 crore, net profit Rs 257 crore (consolidated, facts.md). Sales fell in FY26 (Rs 1,819 crore vs 2,115 crore) and rebounded sharply in FY27 (H1 standalone +45%).
- Last 2 years: about Rs 450 crore spent on Atali (Gujarat) CDMO site, a further Rs 149 crore dedicated block, Xanthine expansion and steroid block debottlenecking. Borrowings rose Rs 413 crore (Mar 2025) to Rs 897 crore (Sep 2026).

## Red flags
- **Suspected: promoter stake fell 3.2 points (42.9% to 39.7%) in the Sep 2026 quarter.** The documents I read do not explain it (sale, gifting, or transfer within the family). Needs a check of the shareholding filing. Source: facts.md.
- **Suspected: CDMO guidance vs presentation.** The Nov ppt chart (p.12) shows CDMO sales of Rs 208 crore (FY26) and "276" for FY27E, which is about +33%, while management guides +40-50% (Rs 291-312 crore) and the Nov call's analyst math implies Rs 400 crore. The ppt labels are garbled in extraction, so verify against the original chart. Sources: docs/2026-11_ppt.txt p.12, docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.8.
- **Suspected: Xanthine capacity stated differently.** Annual report and Sep ppt say 9,000 MTPA; Aug and Nov calls say 9,500-9,600 MTPA. Likely a later add-on, but unexplained. Sources: docs/annual_report_2026.txt, docs/2026-11_ppt.txt p.5.
- **Accounting: auditor Emphasis of Matter** (not a qualification) on an FX target-redemption forward not fair-valued in FY25, found a year late; the FY25 comparative was not restated (Rs 6 crore judged immaterial). Q1 FY27 figures were also restated for FVTPL treatment (ppt p.19 note). A control weakness on hedging, small in amount. Source: docs/annual_report_2026.txt.
- **Cash flow vs profit:** H1 FY27 operating cash flow ~Rs 30 crore against ~Rs 270 crore EBITDA; management's "150-165 day" cycle does not match the 251 days in facts.md.
- Checked, none found: pledge (annual report), qualified opinion, preferential issues/warrants/QIP, material related-party issues.

## Valuation (not scored)
- Price Rs 998, market cap Rs 9,049 crore, P/E 34.8 on TTM consolidated profit of Rs 257 crore (facts.md). About 4.2x TTM sales (Rs 2,161 crore).
- Management gives no profit number, only 15-18% annual growth in revenue and EBITDA for 3-4 years. If FY27 profit is about Rs 280-300 crore (H1 consolidated profit of Rs 147 crore, with H2 guided higher), P/E on FY27 is ~30-32x. With depreciation rising on Rs 300 crore of capitalisation, profit growth may trail EBITDA growth.
- The price assumes roughly 20%+ yearly profit growth for several years, with CDMO reaching hundreds of crores, while ROE is 8.6% and ROCE 10.7% now. System status: fails 3-year profit growth, ROE >= 20% and RS >= 80 (facts.md).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Xanthine capacity nearly doubled and sold at higher realisations; CDMO has 39 commercial projects and a Rs 1,000 crore target with a dedicated block coming; H1 FY27 sales +45% and EBITDA +59% show operating leverage. Capex intensity is guided to fall from FY28.
- **Bear:** H1 strength rides war-elevated raw material and Xanthine prices that management says will come under pressure in the spot segment; cash conversion is poor and debt keeps rising; the promoter stake dropped; the CDMO pipeline count has barely moved in 3 years (20 in development) while the target is 5x the current level.

## I'm wrong if
- (Bearish check) Q3 FY27 standalone OPM falls below 20% while Xanthine realisation falls.
- (Bearish check) Borrowings exceed Rs 1,100 crore by Mar 2027 with operating cash flow below Rs 100 crore for FY27.
- (Bullish check) FY27 CDMO sales below Rs 290 crore (the low end of +40%), meaning the guidance was missed.
- Promoter stake falls below 38% without a stated reason.
- Debtor plus inventory days still above FY26 levels in the Mar 2027 balance sheet.
