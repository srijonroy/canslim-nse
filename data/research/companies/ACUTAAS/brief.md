## The business in 5 lines
Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics, Surat, Gujarat) makes advanced pharma intermediates (generic APIs going off-patent, plus CDMO work for innovator drugs) and specialty chemicals (battery electrolyte additives, semiconductor chemicals via BFC and a new South Korea plant, old commodity chemicals being phased out). It earns by selling intermediates under long-term contracts, mostly exports. FY26 sales Rs 1,339 crore (TTM Rs 1,593 crore), pharma intermediates 91% of H1 FY27 sales. In 2 years OPM went from 18% (Mar 2024) to 39% (TTM) as sales mix moved to CDMO; the CDMO anchor is an intermediate for Darolutamide (Bayer/Orion). Capacity: Ankleshwar CDMO plant Rs 330 crore invested, 30% utilised (docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.7).

## Red flags
No confirmed red flag. Checked: pledge, qualified opinion, emphasis of matter, preferential issues, related-party transactions, contingent liabilities (annual report searches, not a full read).

Suspected / to verify:
- Promoter stake fell from 36% to 32.7% in Jun 2025 (facts.md). I did not find the cause in the documents read (possible sale or transfer). Check the Jun 2025 shareholding filing.
- Working capital drifting up: 99 to 115 days in one quarter on debtors; inventory days 149 at Mar 2026 (facts.md; docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.5). Cash conversion is slower than the profit growth.
- Concentration: the CDMO anchor is an innovator drug intermediate (Darolutamide) and management declines to give any split of CDMO vs core or customer share (docs/2026-11_transcript.txt pp.7, 16). Size of dependence is unknown.
- Management claim vs numbers: "specialty chemicals not to decline" vs H1 -15.6%.
- Document check: Q2 sales Rs 4,369 mn and PAT Rs 1,231 mn agree between transcript, presentation and facts.md. No disagreement found. The market cap in the presentation (Rs 29,208 crore at 30 Sep) differs from facts.md (Rs 33,069 crore) only because of date.

## Valuation (not scored)
- P/E now: 75.3 (facts.md); market cap Rs 33,069 crore; TTM sales Rs 1,593 crore, so about 21x sales; TTM net profit Rs 439 crore.
- FY27 on guidance: sales about Rs 1,740 crore (FY26 Rs 1,339 crore x ~1.30); EBITDA at 38-40% about Rs 660-700 crore; at the H1 PAT margin of 25.8% PAT is about Rs 450-470 crore, giving roughly 70-73x FY27 earnings. My calculation, management gave no PAT guidance.
- Price assumes: to reach a P/E of about 30 on FY27 profit, profit would need to be around 2.4x, that is roughly 25-30% a year for 3-4 years while margins stay near 38-40%. CDMO doubling to Rs 2,000 crore by FY30 plus battery and semiconductor ramps is the path management describes, so the price assumes nearly all of it works and margins hold.
- Market already knows the story (RS 95, FII holding up 7 points in 2 years).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** CDMO crosses Rs 1,000 crore this year and doubles by FY30 with spare capacity (30% utilised) and no new capex; battery and Korea semiconductor revenue add on top from FY28; guidance has been raised twice and looks conservative for H2; net cash, ROCE about 32%.
- **Bear:** 75x earnings leaves no room for a miss; margins of 40% depend on CDMO mix and a few innovator molecules (concentration undisclosed); working capital lengthening; specialty segment shrinking; Korea and electronic-grade chemicals are slow, qualification-driven businesses.

## I'm wrong if
- Q3 FY27 (Dec 2026) sales growth below 25% YoY (Dec 2025 base Rs 393 crore, so below about Rs 491 crore), or FY27 full-year sales growth below 30%.
- EBITDA margin below 35% for two quarters in a row.
- FY27 CDMO revenue (as management describes it) is shown at under Rs 1,000 crore at the Q4 call.
- Working capital goes above 125 days, or debtor days rise again in Q3.
- Promoter holding falls below 30%, or any pledge appears.
- Battery chemicals revenue still not disclosed or not visible in specialty segment sales by Q4 FY27 (specialty segment should be above Rs 40 crore a quarter).
