“I am pleased to report a strong close to FY26, with Q4 
revenue from operations reaching Rs. 4,328 million — up 
40.3% year-on-year — and our highest ever PAT margin of 
31.0%.
Our strategy is clear: build a diversified chemicals business 
across three high-growth verticals — Battery Chemicals, 
Semiconductors, and Pharmaceutical CDMO. By scaling these 
in parallel, we are de-risking our revenue base while creating 
multiple independent engines of compounding, long-term 
growth.
We enter FY27 from a position of strength. I am confident of 
delivering 25% revenue growth in FY27.”
