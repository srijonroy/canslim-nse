“We delivered strong revenue of ₹4,369 million, representing 
42.7% year-on-year growth. The performance was led by the 
continued momentum in our Pharmaceutical Intermediates 
business, which was offset by softer performance in the Specialty 
Chemicals segment. 
A significant milestone during the quarter was the inauguration of 
our South Korea manufacturing facility ahead of schedule. The 
facility is equipped to produce high-purity chemicals used in 
photoresists, semiconductor packaging materials, etching 
materials and other semiconductor applications, positioning us to 
capitalize on opportunities in the fast-growing semiconductor 
ecosystem.
Improved revenue visibility and robust order pipeline gives me 
confidence to revise our FY27 revenue growth guidance upward 
from 25% to around 30%. We are also raising our EBITDA margin 
guidance to 38-40% range.”
