## The business in 5 lines
Chennai Petroleum (CPCL) is a 10.5 MMT/yr standalone refinery at Chennai, a subsidiary of Indian Oil (promoters 67.3%, unchanged for 8 quarters). It earns the refining margin (crack) between crude cost and product prices; about 90-92% of output goes to Indian Oil at refinery transfer price under a long-term agreement (docs/2026-04_transcript.txt p.7, p.16). FY26 sales Rs 63,148 crore, net profit Rs 3,103 crore; TTM sales Rs 76,197 crore, profit Rs 4,174 crore (facts.md). FY26 throughput was a record 11.71 MMT (112% of rated capacity) and GRM was $9.2/bbl against Singapore $5.83 (p.3). Changes in the last 2 years: borrowings cut from Rs 3,117 crore to Rs 1,964 crore, distillate yield up to 79.1%, first-ever interim dividend, and new projects approved (LOBS Group II/III Rs 1,600 crore, 300 retail outlets Rs 400 crore).

## Red flags
- **Confirmed (regulatory): board composition.** Auditor's report says the company "did not have the minimum number of Independent Directors including one Woman Independent Director" required under the Companies Act and SEBI LODR (docs/annual_report_2026.txt, Other Matters; also in the consolidated report). Common among PSUs, but it is a confirmed non-compliance and sets the cap on conviction.
- **Suspected: CBRPL investment.** Auditor's Emphasis of Matter on Note 44.1 (funds deployed in Cauvery Basin Refinery JV). Share warrants of Rs 84.75 crore carried; the project's approval is still pending and the structure is changing to 75% Indian Oil / 25% CPCL, after which CBRPL becomes an Indian Oil subsidiary. CPCL's future equity cheque is unknown.
- **Suspected: Q4 quality.** Reported GRM $13.75 vs core $10.3 (docs/2026-04_transcript.txt p.7); part of the Mar 2026 and likely Jun 2026 profit is inventory gain.
- **Concentration:** 90-92% of sales to the parent at a regulated-style transfer price; government can change this by export duties or RTP adjustments (analysts raised an export-duty/RTP netback issue; management said current net GRM is near long-term average, p.6-8). 30-40% of the Middle East term crude was disrupted for a short period by the Hormuz closure (p.12).
- **Checked, none found:** promoter selling (flat 67.3%), pledge (only a "not applicable" statement), going-concern doubt on CPCL itself, dilution, rising debtor days.
- Not checked: the related-party note in full and Note 33B contingent liabilities amounts (only the existence of disputed statutory dues was seen).

## Valuation (not scored)
- P/E 5.12 on the Screener snapshot (price Rs 1,436, market cap Rs 21,382 crore); at the 2026-10-07 close of Rs 1,612 the market cap is about Rs 24,000 crore, so P/E on TTM profit of Rs 4,174 crore is about 5.8.
- No profit guidance exists, so no forward P/E from management. On the 5-year average profit (FY22-26, about Rs 2,190 crore) the P/E is about 11.
- Market cap is about 0.3x TTM sales; about 2.2x book (Rs 746 per share). Dividend Rs 62 per share for FY26 (interim Rs 8 + final Rs 54), about 3.8% at Rs 1,612; payout was 30% of profit, so the rest stays in the business.
- Plain words: the price assumes profit falls by roughly half from the current TTM level to a mid-cycle figure and stays there. A low P/E on peak margins is normal for refiners and does not by itself mean it is cheap.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Operating performance is best-in-class among Indian refiners (GRM premium to Singapore for 5-6 years, 112% utilisation, record yield), net debt is near zero, and the buyer is fixed. LOBS Group II/III and retail add margin, debottlenecking could add throughput, and a bonus issue or higher payout is possible at a market cap of ~5-6x TTM earnings.
- **Bear:** Earnings are a pure crack play: FY25 profit was Rs 214 crore, FY26 Rs 3,103 crore. FY26-27 includes war-driven cracks and inventory gains. When cracks normalise the profit can fall by more than half, and the stock was cheap at similar P/Es before. Parent controls pricing and capex priorities (CBR). Unit shutdown due around Oct 2026.

## I'm wrong if
- Sep 2026 quarter net profit below Rs 500 crore, or OPM below 4% (cracks back to the FY25 range).
- Reported GRM minus core GRM stays above $3/bbl while core GRM falls below $8/bbl.
- Net borrowings go above Rs 3,000 crore, or CPCL is asked for a large equity infusion into CBRPL.
- Promoter (Indian Oil) stake changes from 67.3% (e.g. an offer for sale / disinvestment), or the Board is still short of independent directors at the next annual report.
- FY27 throughput falls below 10.5 MMT.
- Final-dividend cut: FY27 payout below Rs 30 per share.
