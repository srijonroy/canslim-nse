## The business in 5 lines
Cupid makes male and female condoms, lubricants and rapid diagnostic kits (HIV, Hepatitis B, syphilis, malaria, dengue). It sells to governments and UN agencies abroad (59% of FY26 revenue) and, since FY24, branded consumer products in India (condoms, deodorants, make-up, hand wash).
H1 FY27 revenue was Rs 342.58 crore (+137%), EBITDA Rs 114.83 crore, profit Rs 100.37 crore (+156%). Exports were only 33.4% of H1 revenue; domestic jumped to Rs 224.64 crore, against Rs 121.61 crore for all of FY26 (2026-11_ppt p.6, p.11).
The domestic jump is tied to two retail deals: Rs 331.53 crore committed to Baazar Style Retail (290+ stores) and, from Nov 2026, an exclusive 400 sq ft in each of 425+ V2 Retail stores. Management expects ~Rs 200 crore and ~Rs 400 crore of revenue from them (p.9, p.33).
Palava plant (male condom capacity 480 mn to ~1.25 bn a year) is now due in Q3 FY27 (p.31).
FY27 guidance was raised four times in six months: Rs 600 crore to Rs 880 crore+ revenue, Rs 180 crore to Rs 275 crore+ profit (p.17).

## Red flags
1. **Suspected: investing in a customer.** Rs 331.53 crore (about 74% of Mar-26 equity) goes into Baazar Style through warrants, and the same chain is expected to bring "about Rs 200 crore" of Cupid revenue in FY27. Nothing confirms a related-party link, but the cash may be coming back as sales. Check Baazar Style's purchases from Cupid and receivable terms. Source: 2026-11_ppt p.9, p.16, p.33.
2. **Suspected: unverifiable guidance path.** FY29 profit guidance moved from Rs 390 crore (May) to ~Rs 605 crore (Nov); FY27 revenue went up 47% in six months. Management has held no earnings call since Jan 2024 and takes no analyst questions. Source: 2026-11_ppt p.17.
3. **Quality of Q2 profit:** other income Rs 21.11 crore was 29% of pre-tax profit, while EBITDA fell QoQ. Source: 2026-11_ppt p.15; facts.md.
4. **Working-capital figures differ:** Screener inventory days 350 vs ~70 days from the company's own balance sheet. Probably a calculation basis difference, unconfirmed. Source: facts.md; 2026-11_ppt p.43.
5. **Resolved since the last brief:** the "FMCG Rs 121.61 crore vs Rs 84.26 crore" mismatch is two different measures (domestic consumer revenue vs new FMCG products only; p.41). The promoter pledge is now "negligible" (p.45, company statement; not independently verified).

Checked and clean: promoter holding (rising, not selling), no qualified audit opinion / emphasis of matter (annual report, earlier brief).

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap Rs 50,801 crore at Rs 378 (facts.md). On TTM profit Rs 169 crore: P/E ~300. On management's FY27 profit guidance (Rs 275 crore+): ~185x. On FY28 (Rs 390 crore): ~130x. On FY29 (Rs 605 crore): ~84x. About 91x TTM sales of Rs 556 crore.
The price assumes management hits every number through FY29, with profit growing about 3.5x in three years, and then keeps growing. The stock was Rs 16,301 crore in May 2026, so it has roughly tripled in six months along with the guidance upgrades. Hitting the guidance may not be enough to hold the price if the multiple falls.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Rare WHO-prequalified supplier with 33% EBITDA margins, contracted multi-year supply (South Africa, PFSCM), capacity about 2.6x from Q3 FY27, and a consumer push into 700+ value-retail stores. Management beat every number since 2023, and the promoter keeps buying.
- **Bear:** At ~185x FY27 profit, any miss hurts. The H2 target needs ~1.5x H1 profit, and a lot of it rests on retail partners the company is financing. Q2 margins and EBITDA already slipped, other income props up profit, Palava is again late, and there are no analyst calls to test any of this.

## I'm wrong if
- Q3 FY27 revenue below Rs 230 crore (H2 needs Rs 475 crore+ of revenue for Rs 880 crore), or Q3 EBITDA margin below 28%.
- Palava is not commissioned by 31 Mar 2027.
- Receivables or inventory grow faster than sales in the March 2027 balance sheet, or Baazar Style/V2 receivables become a big share of debtors.
- Borrowings exceed Rs 200 crore, or the promoter pledge goes up again.
- FY27 guidance is cut, or promoter holding falls below 45%.

## Questions only you can answer (scuttlebutt)

- Is Baazar Style a related party of the promoters or of Cupid's management? Look at its annual report: purchases from Cupid, who owns it, and what it pays for the warrants.
