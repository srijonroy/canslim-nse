Mr. Aditya Kumar Halwasiya,
Chairman & Managing Director
“H1 FY27 reflects strong business momentum: operating income grew 137% to ₹342.58
crore and net profit rose 156% to ₹100.37 crore. Domestic Consumer Healthcare & FMCG
contributed 49% of H1 revenue, alongside our international business, which continues to
benefit from institutional partnerships.
Our path-breaking associations with Style Baazar and V2 Retail take the Cupid brand
and distribution to the next level. Style Baazar is expected to contribute about ₹200
crore in FY27, and V2 Retail offers an estimated ₹400 crore opportunity over the next 12
months.
We expect H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1, as Palava is to be commissioned in Q3 and
both partnerships scale up. We have raised FY27 guidance to at least ₹880 crore
revenue and ₹275 crore+ net profit. Multiple associations are being worked upon, and we
will make the Company debt-free as we go along.
