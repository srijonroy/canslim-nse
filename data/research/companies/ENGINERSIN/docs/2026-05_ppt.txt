Oil & Refining Capacity Outlook
Global oil demand projected to exceed ~106 mb/d by 2026.
India expected to remain among the largest contributors to incremental global oil demand growth.
India’s oil demand projected to rise from ~5.5 mb/d in 2024 to ~6.7 mb/d by 2030.
India’s refining capacity targeted to increase from ~258 MMTPA to ~310–320 MMTPA by 2030.
Indian Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) and private refiners are investing over INR 2 lakh crore in refinery expansion, petrochemical 
integration and cleaner fuel projects.
India achieved ~20% ethanol blending target ahead of schedule in several regions
Biofuels, SAF, CBG, green hydrogen and refinery-petrochemical integration expected to drive future investments.

Gas Economy and Energy Transition
India targets increasing natural gas share in energy mix from ~6% to 15% by 2030.

LNG imports expected to more than double by 2030.

National Green Hydrogen Mission targets production of ~5 MMTPA green hydrogen by 2030. 
Non-fossil fuel installed electricity capacity has already crossed ~220 GW.
