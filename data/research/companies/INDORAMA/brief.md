## The business in 5 lines
Indo Rama Synthetics (India) makes polyester: staple fibre, filament yarn (POY/DTY/FDY), chips and PET resin, at one integrated plant at Butibori, Nagpur (672,000 TPA cumulative; PET resin 650 TPD). It is a B2B commodity converter: buys PTA and MEG (crude-linked) and sells fibre/yarn to textile makers, PET resin to bottlers. FY26 consolidated sales Rs 4,910 crore, net profit Rs 150 crore (FY25: Rs 1.4 crore); TTM sales Rs 4,661 crore, profit Rs 211 crore. 74.8-75% owned by Indorama Ventures (Thailand), which also supplies raw material. Last 2 years: loss in FY24, then recovery as margins rose from -0.9% to 8% (TTM) and debt fell from Rs 1,339 crore to Rs 861 crore.

## Valuation (not scored)
P/E 12 (facts.md); market cap Rs 2,567 crore vs TTM sales Rs 4,661 crore (about 0.55x) and TTM profit Rs 211 crore. No management profit guidance exists. At this price the market assumes FY26-TTM profit is roughly sustainable and does not pay for growth; a return to FY24-type margins would erase the profit. Price at Rs 97.6 near the 52-week high of 112.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Margin recovery continues, PET resin utilisation rises with anti-dumping duty, debt keeps falling (861 from 1,339), and at P/E 12 any rerating adds to earnings growth.
- **Bear:** Pure commodity spread business under Chinese overcapacity; profit was near zero in FY25 and a loss in FY24. Promoter-controlled input supply, no institutions, no guidance or concalls to monitor.

## I'm wrong if
- OPM falls below 6% for two consecutive quarters.
- Borrowings rise back above Rs 1,140 crore.
- Promoter stake falls or any pledge appears.
- Sales for a quarter fall below Rs 1,000 crore again (as in Jun 2026, Rs 937 crore) without a margin offset.
