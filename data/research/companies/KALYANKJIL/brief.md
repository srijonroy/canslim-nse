## The business in 5 lines
- Kalyan Jewellers is a pan-India gold and studded jewellery retailer. It has 1,203 showrooms in total (about 2 in the UK, the rest India and Middle East) and makes its money from jewellery sales margin, with about 60% of sales on weddings (Nov call p.10).
- Consolidated sales for FY26 were Rs 35,743 crore (TTM Rs 41,530 crore). Net profit was Rs 1,350 crore for FY26 (TTM Rs 1,602 crore). Operating margin is thin at 6-7% (facts.md).
- Growth comes from franchise-owned, company-operated (FOCO) stores. Management opens 70-80 a year, so the model is asset-light (Aug call p.6).
- In the last two years sales grew 38% a year (Aug call p.6). Candere (online) turned profitable in H2 FY26. The Tamil Nadu regional brand ATM launched in Aug 2026. Non-GML debt is cleared.
- Old-gold exchange is now more than 40% of sales (Nov call p.6), and gold-for-cash buying was introduced. Customs-duty inventory gains lifted Q1 and Q2 profit.

## Red flags
- **Suspected: related-party concerns.** The Aug call includes an analyst question citing Moneylife and some mutual funds on RPTs. Management declined to answer (docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.6). I found nothing confirming a violation in the annual report, so it is suspected, not confirmed. Please check the RPT note independently.
- **Suspected: profit quality.** H1 reported PAT of Rs 776 crore against adjusted Rs 651 crore (Nov call p.4-5), with Sep 2026 other income at 27% of pre-tax profit.
- **Watch: rising employee cost.** Standalone employee cost rose about 54% in Q1; management says it "should be there in the future quarters also" (Aug call p.13).
- Checked and clean: no pledge, no qualified opinion or emphasis of matter, no preferential allotment or QIP, no convertible instruments (annual report). Promoters were flat at 62.9%.

## Valuation (not scored)
- P/E is 45.7 on TTM profit of Rs 1,602 crore. Market cap is Rs 71,983 crore, about 1.7x TTM sales.
- Management gives no profit guidance. If I take its "25% revenue growth" and "flat PBT margin", FY27 profit would be about Rs 1,700-1,800 crore (my estimate, not a company figure), which is about 40x.
- The price assumes about 25% profit growth for 3+ years with margins held. Adjusted profit is lower than reported, so the real P/E is somewhat higher.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Asset-light FOCO rollout of 70-80 stores a year plus ATM and Candere, with the 60% unorganised share shifting to organised. The non-South share is rising, volumes are growing, and non-GML debt is gone.
- **Bear:** Thin 6-7% operating margin means a small slip hits profit hard. Reported profit includes duty gains. Q3 and Q4 face a very high base. Gold-price swings, discounting and employee cost pressure remain, and the RPT question was not answered.

## I'm wrong if
- Q3 FY27 consolidated sales (ex-bullion) growth is below 25%, or same-store growth falls below 10%.
- Consolidated operating margin falls below 6% for two quarters, or India PBT margin (ex-bullion) is below the FY26 level of about 5.5%.
- Borrowings rise above Rs 7,500 crore without matching sales growth, or inventory days rise above 180.
- Promoter holding falls below 60% or any pledge appears.
