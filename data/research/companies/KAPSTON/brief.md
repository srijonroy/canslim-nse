## The business in 5 lines
- Hyderabad-based manpower contractor (since 2009): general staffing, security guarding, integrated facility management, engineering and IT staffing. 30,000+ people deployed, 700+ clients (presentation) / 800+ (annual report) across pharma, manufacturing, IT, healthcare, logistics (docs/2026-06_ppt.txt p.2, 15; annual_report_2026.txt p.6).
- Money comes from billing clients for deployed staff plus a small margin; OPM is only 5-6.6% and revenue is labour pass-through.
- Size: FY26 sales Rs 830 crore, net profit Rs 28 crore; TTM sales Rs 861 crore, profit Rs 30 crore (facts.md).
- Last 2 years: sales up 26% a year, profit up ~50% a year (docs/2026-06_ppt.txt p.24); OPM rose from 4.3% (Sep 2024) to 6.6% (Jun 2026). 1:2 bonus issue in early 2026; new B2C home-services subsidiary set up Feb 2026.
- Receivables are 58.8% of total assets (annual report, auditor's key audit matter), funded by bank debt rated CRISIL BBB/Stable.

## Valuation (not scored)
- Price Rs 550 (547 on 2026-10-06), market cap Rs 1,675 cr, trailing P/E 54.9 (facts.md). On FY26 EPS of Rs 9.24 (annual report) it is about 59x.
- Market cap is 1.9x TTM sales (Rs 861 cr). With debt of Rs 184 cr (March 2026), enterprise value is roughly Rs 1,860 cr, about 38x TTM operating profit of Rs 49 cr.
- Management gave no profit guidance for next year, so no forward P/E can be taken from them. For reference: at TTM profit of Rs 30 cr, reaching a P/E of 25 on today's market cap needs profit of about Rs 67 cr, 2.2x today's.
- Plain words: the price assumes profit roughly doubling in about 3 years (about 30% a year), which means sales growth staying near 20-25% and OPM climbing from 6.6% toward 8%, in a low-margin business with bank-funded receivables.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Steady 20%+ sales growth, OPM up from 4.3% to 6.6% in six quarters, and profit growing faster than sales. Earnings quality is good (little other income). Promoter holds 72.9% with no dilution beyond a bonus. Operating leverage could continue as headcount scales.
- **Bear:** A thin-margin labour-contracting business with no visible moat, ~90 debtor days and rising debt, valued at 55x trailing earnings. Q1 FY27 growth slowed to 16%. Home services is a distraction, and without concalls there is no guidance to hold management to.

## I'm wrong if
- Sales growth on year stays below 15% for the Sep 2026 and Dec 2026 quarters (Jun 2026 was +16%).
- OPM falls back below 5.5% (Jun 2026: 6.6%).
- Debtor days rise above 100, or borrowings exceed Rs 220 cr without a matching rise in profit.
- Promoter stake falls below 70% or any pledge appears.
- Home Services spending shows up as a visible loss in consolidated results.
