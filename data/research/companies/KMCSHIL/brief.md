## The business in 5 lines
- KMC Speciality Hospitals runs one hospital campus in Trichy, Tamil Nadu: a 250-bed legacy block and a 200-bed Mother & Child block (Maa Kauvery, opened 29 Jan 2024). Reported census (operating) beds are 330 (docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.24).
- It earns from inpatient and outpatient fees (tertiary care: neuro, gastro, mother and child, transplants). ~89% of inpatients come from Trichy and nearby districts (p.6).
- 75% owned by Sri Kauvery Medical Care (India) Ltd, which runs 10 more hospitals (2,574 beds in all). Free float 25%.
- FY26: sales Rs 306 cr (+32%), EBITDA Rs 93 cr, PAT Rs 47 cr (p.20). Q1 FY27: sales Rs 92 cr (+38%), EBITDA margin 32.4%, PAT Rs 16.6 cr (+120%).
- What changed: the new block took occupancy from 68% (FY25) to 86% (Q1 FY27); margins rose from 25.6% to 32%; the company moved from Rs 89 cr debt to net cash.

## Red flags
- **Confirmed:** none. Checked: auditor opinion (clean, no KAM), contingent liabilities (nil claims), promoter holding (75% flat), pledge of shares (hits relate only to property pledged for bank loans), equity issuance (none), convertibles (none).
- **Suspected, bed count:** presentation says "450-bedded" hospital (p.5) but census beds are 330 (p.24). Likely licensed vs operating beds, but not explained in the documents read.
- **Suspected, related party:** Rs 65 cr proposed ceiling of transactions with the parent for FY27 (notice Item 3). Actual FY26 amounts were not extracted in this run. Doctor-services sharing with the parent is also in the notice. Needs a check of the actual RPT note.
- **Minor, definitions:** the presentation shows ROCE 33% for Q1 against 26.2% in facts.md (Screener); the company's ROCE definition differs.
- Group ROCE history shows FY25 dip (17% in facts.md) when the new block was filling.

## Valuation (not scored)
- Market cap Rs 2,782 cr at Rs 171. P/E 49.9 on TTM PAT (facts.md). TTM sales Rs 331 cr gives about 8.4x sales.
- On Q1 FY27 PAT of Rs 16.6 cr annualised (Rs 66 cr), P/E is about 42. No management profit guidance exists, so no P/E on guidance.
- The price assumes profit keeps growing 25-30% a year for 3+ years while holding a 30%+ margin. With 86% occupancy and no announced beds, growth beyond FY28 must come mainly from price (ARPOB) or new capacity.
- Book value Rs 12.9; the price is about 13x book.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Volumes up 25-30% a year, net cash, ROE 29%. Unopened beds (450 vs 330 census) and a 2,574-bed parent give room to grow. Margins still rising.
- **Bear:** Single-city single-site hospital near full. Margins are at a record, valuation (P/E 50) leaves little room, free float is 25% with no institutions, and the parent controls both related-party flows and expansion decisions.

## I'm wrong if
- Occupancy stays above 85% for two more quarters and no new beds or site are announced.
- EBITDA margin falls below 28% in any quarter of FY27-28.
- Quarterly sales growth drops below 20% year on year.
- Related-party dealings with the parent exceed the Rs 65 cr ceiling or grow faster than sales.
- Promoter holding falls below 70% or any share pledge appears.
