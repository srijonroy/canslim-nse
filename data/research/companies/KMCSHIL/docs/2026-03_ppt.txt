Performance Highlights and Commentary 
• Total Income has increased by INR 20.6 Cr. (33%) compared to the Q3 previous year;
• This was achieved on the back of strong-performance across of all our specialties with increasing trend seen
in volumes and revenue mix;
• Consistent strong performance of 27% on Total Income in Q3 FY’26 in our Mother and Child Care services.
• Total EBITDA earned has increased by INR 8.7 Cr (50%) compared to the Q3 previous year;
• EBITDA as a % of revenue for Q3 FY’26 is 31% as compared to 27% in the Q3 previous year;
• PBT & PATas a % of revenue for Q3 FY’26 is 22.2% & 16.5% respectively as compared to 16.1% & 12.0% in
the Q3 previous year.
