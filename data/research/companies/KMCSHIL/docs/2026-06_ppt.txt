Performance Highlights and Commentary 
• Total Income has increased by INR 22.4 Cr. (36%) compared to the Q4 previous year;
• This was achieved on the back of strong-performance across of all our specialties with increasing trend seen
in volumes and revenue mix;
• Consistent strong performance of 26% on Total Income in Q4 FY’26 in our Mother and Child Care services.
• Total EBITDA earned has increased by INR 11.6 Cr (71%) compared to the Q4 previous year;
• EBITDA as a % of revenue for Q4 FY’26 is 33% as compared to 26% in the Q4 previous year;
• PBT & PAT as a % of revenue for Q4 FY’26 is 23.5% & 17.4% respectively as compared to 14.3% & 7.3% in
the Q4 previous year.
