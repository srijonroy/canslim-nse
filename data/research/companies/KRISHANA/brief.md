## The business in 5 lines
Krishana Phoschem (Bhilwara, Rajasthan; Ostwal group) makes phosphatic fertilisers: **SSP** (single super phosphate) and **NPK/DAP** complex fertilisers. It also **trades imported fertilisers** (about a third of Q1 revenue) (2026-07 transcript p.2–5).
Revenue went from ₹323 crore (FY23) to ₹2,416 crore (FY26) and profit from ₹27 crore to ₹180 crore; Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹532 crore (+35%) and profit ₹47 crore (+54%) (facts.md; 2026-07 transcript p.3).
New NPK-DAP capacity (+165,000 tonnes, total 495,000 t) was commissioned around March 2026, but ran at only **43% utilisation** in Q1 because imported raw materials were short (West Asia / Hormuz disruption) (2026-07 transcript p.3–4).
The business runs on India's **nutrient-based subsidy (NBS)**: the government pays part of the price, often late, so receivables and debt are high (2026-04 transcript; annual_report_2026 risks).

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap about ₹5,800 crore (₹188 a share). On TTM profit of ₹197 crore that's a **P/E of about 30**; if FY27 revenue grows 30–35% at an 8–9% PAT margin (about ₹260–290 crore), about **20–22× forward**. **The cheapest stock on the list**, partly because of debt, subsidy dependence and related-party exposure.

## Red flags
1. **Related-party approval with the parent company** (Ostwal Phoschem (India) Ltd) of up to **₹1,400 crore**, in the same business, buying and selling raw materials between group companies. Disclosed and put to a shareholder vote, but large enough to move profit between group companies → conviction capped at 2.
2. **Debt** grew from ₹21 crore to ₹733 crore, with an enabling resolution for further issue of securities (possible dilution).
3. **Receivables / cash flow**: about ₹700 crore of receivables at Mar-26 and negative operating cash flow in FY26.
**Checked and clean:** promoter pledge (none found), audit opinion (clean), promoter stake (rising).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** A new plant just came on, a better NPK mix lifts margins, a P/E around 20× forward, the promoter is buying, and CRISIL A+.
- **Bear:** A regulated, subsidy-dependent, import-dependent business with fast-rising debt, a large related-party channel with the parent, a third of revenue in low-margin trading, and growth guidance already trimmed once.

## I'm wrong if
- Q2 FY27 revenue is below ₹500 crore, or NPK-DAP utilisation stays below 60%.
- Borrowings go above ₹900 crore, or a QIP / preferential issue happens at a discount.
- Related-party transactions with OPIL turn out to be a large share of purchases or sales (check note on related parties in the FY27 half-year results).
- Subsidy rates are cut or payments are delayed by more than 6 months.
