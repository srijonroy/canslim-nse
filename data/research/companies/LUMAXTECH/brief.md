## The business in 5 lines
Lumax Auto Technologies (D.K. Jain group, Delhi) makes auto components across five divisions: **Advanced Plastics** (interiors, mainly the former IAC India, now fully owned and merged), **Mechatronics** (sensors, telematics, ECUs, antennas through JVs with Yokowo, Alps Alpine, Ituran, FAE), **Structures & Control** (gear shifters with Mannoh, frames), **Alternate Fuels** (Greenfuel CNG/LPG kits, acquired Nov 2024), and **Aftermarket** (2026-08 transcript p.3–4; annual_report_2026).
FY26 revenue was ₹4,870 crore (+34%), EBITDA ₹705 crore (14.5%), PAT ₹337 crore; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,364 crore (+33%), EBITDA margin 15.1%, PAT ₹99 crore (+83%) (2026-06 transcript p.2–3; 2026-08 transcript p.2).
Customer mix: passenger vehicles 53%, two- and three-wheelers 24%, aftermarket 10%, CVs 9% (2026-06 transcript).
Plan ("20.20.20.20"): revenue growing 20% a year to ₹10,000 crore+ by FY31, with EBITDA margin moving toward 20% over 5–7 years (2026-08 transcript p.3).

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap about ₹13,500 crore (₹1,979 a share). On TTM profit of ~₹330 crore attributable to shareholders (Screener P/E 42.7 at ₹2,077) that's a **P/E of about 41**. If FY27 grows 20–25% at a 15% margin, about **33× forward**.
The price assumes the 20% growth plan holds through an auto cycle.

## Red flags
None confirmed. **Watch:** the related-party limit of up to ₹675 crore with sister company Lumax Industries (group purchases, sales, royalty, rent). It's disclosed and shareholder-approved, and common in Indian auto groups, but check the actual amounts in the annual report's AOC-2 each year.
**Checked and clean:** pledge (none found), promoter stake (steady 56%), audit opinion (emphasis of matter only about merger accounting).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Management under-promises and beats, an order book of ₹1,600 crore stretches to FY29, growing electronics content, a 20-day cash cycle, acquisition debt being repaid from strong cash flow, and FIIs adding.
- **Bear:** Auto parts are cyclical and management already flags slower H2 growth. Margins are average, and minority partners take a slice. Group related-party flows with Lumax Industries are large. At about 33× forward earnings, a downturn in car sales would hurt.

## I'm wrong if
- H2 FY27 revenue growth falls below 10% and FY27 growth is under 18%.
- EBITDA margin drops below 13.5%.
- Total borrowings rise above ₹1,400 crore without a new acquisition explaining it.
- Related-party transactions with Lumax Industries grow faster than revenue.
