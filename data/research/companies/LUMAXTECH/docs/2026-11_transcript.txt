Q2 FY 27, supported by improved affordability following the GST (Goods and Services Tax) 
2.0, a resilient rural and urban demand, healthy freight movement, infrastructure-led activity and 
a gradual recovery in the replacement cycle.

 Overall industry production grew by 19% on a y-o-y basis to 106 lakh units. Passenger Vehicle 
(PV) production grew by 25% y-o-y to around 17 lakh units. 2-Wheeler production also

 Looking ahead, we do expect growth to moderate somewhat in H2 FY 27 as the industry moves 
past the lower base of H1 and the full benefit of GST rate cuts gets reflected in demand. The

demand momentum sustains. At the same time, the ability of manufacturers to manage rising 
input costs and pass them on to customers will remain important for profitability. 
 Speaking of the performance of the Company, we have delivered another strong quarter with 
healthy growth in revenue and profitability. Q2 FY 27 revenue stood at INR 1,556 crore, up by 
35% y-o-y and 14% quarter-on-quarter (q-o-q). EBITDA grew by 34% y-o-y to INR 228 crore, 
with a margin of 14.7%, broadly stable on a y-o-y basis. The Profit After Tax (PAT) for the 
quarter stood at INR 117 crore, registering strong growth of 50% y-o-y. 
 For H1 FY 27, revenue stood at INR 2,919 crore, up 34% y-o-y. EBITDA grew by 42% to INR 
434 crore, with margins of 14.9%, an expansion of 90 bps (basis points), while PAT grew by 
64% to INR 215 crore. 
 Coming to the order book, it has grown to INR 1,879 crore at the end of Q2, up from INR 1,600 
crore at the end of Q1 FY 27, providing a healthy visibility for the business going forward. Of 
this order book, approximately 14% is expected to be executed in FY 27 itself, 59% in FY 28 
and the remaining 27% in FY 29. 
 Advanced Plastics continues to contribute the largest share at 44%, followed by Mechatronics 
at 27%, Alternate Fuels at 20% and Structures & Control Systems at 8%. Around 85% of the 
order book is new business. By powertrain, EV (Electric Vehicle) and CNG (Compressed 
Natural Gas) together accounts for 29% of the order book, with CNG at 20% and pure EVs at 
about 9%, while new future technologies account for 22% of the total order book. 
 At Lumax Greenfuel Energy Solutions, we are pleased to report that the Nashik facility has 
commenced production in Q3 FY 27, the ongoing quarter, supplying ferrule-less tubes and 
fittings and receptacles to Mahindra & Mahindra. 
 At its meeting on 24th October, 2026, the Board approved the setting up of a new Mechatronics 
manufacturing facility at Manesar in Haryana, with a capital outlay of around INR 150 crore to
