## The business in 5 lines
Manorama Industries (Raipur/Mahasamund) turns sal, mango and shea seeds and butters into cocoa butter equivalents (CBE), stearin and other specialty fats for chocolate, confectionery and cosmetics makers (Ferrero, Mondelez, Nestle, Hershey, L'Oreal on its client slide, docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.20).
Revenue FY26 Rs 1,367 crore (consolidated), up 77% from Rs 771 crore; TTM Rs 1,481 crore, OPM 26-27%, TTM profit Rs 258 crore (facts.md). Exports are 60% of sales (docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.4).
Stearin and CBE are 71.4% of FY26 revenue (docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.6). Fractionation capacity went from 15,000 tons (FY22) to 47,500 tons now, 52,000 expected in FY27 (ppt p.12).
Last 2 years: capacity jump (25,000-ton plant commercialised Jul 2025, +7,500 tons debottlenecking Mar 2026), Rs 500 crore QIP at Rs 1,470 (Jul 2026), a Rs 460 crore capex plan (Indian fractionation/CBA/refinery plus a Burkina Faso seed plant), new subsidiaries in Africa, Brazil (Dekel tie-up) and Chad.
Sales grew 39.5% year on year in Q1 FY27, slower than the 77% of FY26 as the base catches up; sequential growth has slowed to 3% (Rs 391 crore to Rs 404 crore).

## Red flags
Suspected (not confirmed):
- **Supplier quality loss (Aug 2026).** On the 14 Aug call an analyst asks about a disclosure "posted day before yesterday": the supplier's liability and a debit note raised, "claiming the full amount" from the manufacturer. Management gave no figure and no recovery timeline (docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.12-13). The exchange filing is not in my document set; size and product unknown.
- **Promoter stake / dilution.** Promoters 57.3% (Sep 2024) to 54.4% (Dec 2024), then flat at 54.3% (facts.md). Cause not checked (block sale or issue?). The Jul 2026 QIP dilutes it further.
- **Standalone vs consolidated figures differ:** FY26 EBITDA Rs 367.7 crore at 27.1% standalone (docs/2026-05_transcript.txt p.3) vs 26.4% consolidated (docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.5) and Rs 361 crore in facts.md; probably the basis, but worth a check. On raw material, management says about 50% of sales while an analyst cited 75-80% in H1 (docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.9).
- **Subsidiaries:** consolidated losses of Rs 8 crore in Q4 FY26 and Rs 3 crore in Q1 FY27 on almost no revenue; the Africa entities are cost centres (docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.10-11).
- **Forex:** Rs 23.3 crore mark-to-market provision in FY26 on forward contracts; 50-60% of exposure hedged (docs/2026-05_transcript.txt p.3; docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.17). Profit moves with the rupee.

Checked and found nothing: no qualified opinion or key audit matter in the standalone auditor's report, no pledge of shares, no preferential allotment or QIP in FY26 itself, contingent liabilities about Rs 1 crore (annual report note 30). No flag is confirmed, so no cap applies.

## Valuation (not scored)
- P/E 45.1 on TTM profit Rs 258 crore at Rs 1,853 (facts.md); the stock closed at Rs 1,985.60 on 6 Oct 2026, about 48x TTM. On Q1 annualised profit (Rs 79 crore x 4 = Rs 316 crore) it is roughly 40x, before about 5% share dilution from the QIP.
- Market cap about Rs 11,650-12,500 crore against TTM sales Rs 1,481 crore: roughly 8x sales.
- Management gave no profit or revenue guidance for FY27 ("you can extract the numbers", docs/2026-08_transcript.txt p.12). My own estimate: sales up 30% to about Rs 1,780 crore at 26% OPM gives profit near Rs 310-340 crore, about 37x.
- The price assumes about 25-30% profit growth for 3+ years, capex delivered on time and margin held at 25-27%. It leaves little room for a margin slip or a slow FY28 (no new capacity until Q3 FY28).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** FY27 growth is covered by capacity already built (80% of 52,000 tons plus 5-10% price), 26% margins and 35% ROCE, sticky Fortune 500 clients, and the FY28 capex adds forward integration (CBA, refinery) and cheaper sourcing (Burkina Faso, 3-year payback).
- **Bear:** Sequential growth slowed to 3% in Q1, growth after FY27 depends on Rs 460 crore of plants that start Q3 FY28, inventory is Rs 710 crore against Rs 1,367 crore sales with working capital set to rise, forex and raw-material swings move profit, and 40-48x already assumes all of it.

## I'm wrong if
- Q2 FY27 sales below Rs 400 crore (flat on Q1) or year-on-year growth under 25% by Q3 FY27.
- Operating margin below 23% for two quarters.
- Inventory above Rs 800 crore or borrowings back above Rs 500 crore before the new plants earn anything.
- The 4,500-ton debottlenecking slips past Q3 FY27, or Burkina Faso approvals / Q3 FY28 commissioning slip again.
- The supplier loss is material (my guess: above Rs 20 crore) or not recovered, or the promoter stake falls below 50%.
