MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY – Q2 & H1FY27​
4
DEEP SARAF
Managing Director
“We are pleased to report another strong quarter, with Q2FY27 revenue growing 41.9% YoY to 
₹4,589 million, EBITDA rising 34.4% to ₹1,182 million and PAT increasing 51.8% to ₹808 
million. H1FY27 performance remained equally robust, with revenue growth of 40.8% and PAT 
growth of 59.2%, driven by healthy demand, a stronger value-added mix and disciplined 
execution.
We are now focused on translating our expanded capacities into sustainable growth, supported 
by an increasingly diversified global customer base, with exports accounting for 61% of 
H1FY27 revenue.
The leadership transition strengthens Manorama Industries governance and management 
architecture, bringing greater clarity, accountability and professional control as the business 
scales. The new structure creates a stronger institutional foundation to execute growth plans, 
deepen global relationships and create sustainable long-term value.
Innovation remains a critical growth lever, anchored by our DSIR-certified Milcoa® Innovation & 
Research Centre and customer-led product development capabilities. With an integrated 
business model, stronger sourcing capabilities, an expanding value-added portfolio, growing 
global reach and robust balance sheet, we believe Manorama is well positioned to capture the 
long-term opportunities across CBE and specialty fats and deliver sustainable stakeholder 
value.”

Demand Tailwinds Powering CBE & Specialty Fats Adoption
18
Strong demand drivers underpin Manorama’s integrated specialty fats proposition
1 Growing Adoption of CBE
• CBE is a trans-fat-free vegetable fat fully compatible 
with cocoa butter, offering similar physical and 
chemical properties
2
Need for Heat – Stable Chocolate 
Products in Tropical Regions
3
Increasing Demand for Natural & 
Sustainable Cosmetic Ingredients 4 Regulatory Support Driving CBE 
Adoption
• CBE improves heat and melt stability, helping 
chocolates retain quality in warm climates. 
• Extended shelf life and better storage performance 
make CBE particularly suitable for tropical markets 
such as India, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
• Shea, Sal and Mango butters are widely used in 
moisturisers, skincare, lip care and premium personal 
care formulations
• Consumer preference for plant-based and naturally 
sourced ingredients is driving demand for specialty 
cosmetic oils and butters.
• In the European Union, India (FSSAI), and 
several other markets, manufacturers are allowed 
to use up to 5% CBE in the finished chocolate 
product while retaining the ‘chocolate’ label
