I N V E S T M E N T  C R I T E R I A
Owned Under a Framework Built for Trust
Fit & Proper
All shareholders must be assessed and 
remain 'fit and proper' at all times.
Prior SEBI Approval
Shareholders seeking to acquire beyond 5% 
require prior approval from SEBI.
51% Public Holding
At least 51% of shareholding must be held by 
the public at all times.
49% Trading-Member Cap
Aggregate holding of trading members & 
associates must not exceed 49%.
15% Foreign-Entity Cap
Foreign exchanges, depositories, banks or 
insurers may hold up to 15%.
Structural Safeguards
Limits protect neutrality, prevent 
concentration and safeguard market 
integrity.
