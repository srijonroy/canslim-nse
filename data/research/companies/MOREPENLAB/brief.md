## The business in 5 lines
- Makes bulk drugs (APIs, ~53% of sales, 72% exported; market leader in loratadine, desloratadine, montelukast), finished-dose Rx/OTC (~20%, Burnol etc.) and medical devices (~27%: glucometers, BP monitors, number 1 in India) (2025-05_transcript p.3).
- Sales Rs 1,806 crore FY26 (flat on FY25's 1,812), TTM Rs 1,951 crore; operating margin 7-10% for five years, Q1 FY27 jumped to 14% (facts.md).
- What changed: Rs 825 crore (USD 91 mn) multi-year CDMO mandate from "a leading global pharma company"; commercial supply started June 2026, Rs 58 crore dispatched in Q1 FY27 (2026-08_ppt p.3-4; annual_report_2026 p.14).
- Q1 FY27: sales Rs 575 crore (+34% YoY), EBITDA Rs 87.7 crore (+207%), PAT Rs 56 crore (+394%) off a weak base (2026-08_ppt p.4, p.7).
- Also: Dr. Morepen (consumer/generic distribution) cut from 80% to 19.94% and deconsolidated Jul 2025; devices business being hived off into a subsidiary (annual_report_2026, Emphasis of Matter and AOC-2).

## Red flags
- **Earnings-quality (confirmed):** FY26 profit includes a gain of ~Rs 24.7 crore from losing control of Dr. Morepen (cash flow statement). Other income was 65% of PBT in Sep 2025. Operating profit FY26 was Rs 126 crore vs 174 crore, a 28% fall. This caps the conviction at 2.
- **Auditor Emphasis of Matter (confirmed):** the auditor drew attention to the deconsolidation of Dr. Morepen and its two step-down subsidiaries (annual_report_2026 p.108). Not a qualification.
- **Related party / restructuring (confirmed, scale unclear):** the medical devices business is to be transferred on slump sale to Morepen Medipath for Rs 181 crore, approved Jan 2025, still not completed and its deadline extended to the FY27 AGM; devices assets are shown as "held for sale" in the accounts. Devices is ~27% of sales and the most reliable growth segment, so the structure and who owns it matters.
- **Suspected, minor:** CDMO mandate quoted as Rs 823 crore in 2026-05_ppt and Rs 825 crore in 2026-08_ppt and annual report; likely rounding. First dispatch "~Rs 50 crore" (annual report) vs "Rs 58 crore" (Aug 2026 ppt): different dates.
- **Checked, not found:** promoter pledge (one hit, which is an unrelated audit statement), qualified opinion, going-concern doubt, convertible instruments or warrants outstanding (none, annual_report_2026 p.152).
- Not checked: contingent liabilities detail and full related-party notes (searched, hits were policy text only).

## Valuation (not scored)
- Market cap Rs 6,904 crore; P/E 58.8 (facts.md snapshot); ~3.5x TTM sales of Rs 1,951 crore. TTM PAT Rs 141 crore gives about 49x on my division of the same two numbers; the 58.8 likely uses a different profit base.
- No profit guidance exists for FY27 (management says it "does not give guidance"). If Q1 FY27 PAT of Rs 56 crore repeated four times (Rs 225 crore), the P/E would be about 31x. That assumes the 15% margin holds and ignores how much of Q1 was the base-effect rebound.
- Plain words: the price assumes margins stay near 14-15% (against 7-10% for five years) and the CDMO order ramps, for several years. ROE is 5% and ROCE is 8%, so the price is paying for the future, not the present.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** CDMO supply lifts margins permanently from 7-10% to 14%+ with 800 to 1,200 KL of capacity behind it; API prices recover after the 24% fall; devices grow with 14 mn+ installed meters feeding a strip consumables stream. Four clean USFDA inspections support more CDMO orders.
- **Bear:** One quarter of 15% margin sits against five years of 7-10%, with a history of missed growth and margin guidance and capacity dates pushed back. A single-customer CDMO contract with an unstated duration. FY26 profit was helped by a one-off. At ~59x P/E with falling institutional holding, there is little room for a miss.

## I'm wrong if
- Q2 FY27 operating margin falls back below 10% while CDMO dispatches are continuing.
- Q2/Q3 FY27 CDMO dispatches stay at or below ~Rs 58 crore per quarter (would imply a long, slow ramp), or no second CDMO customer is named by FY27 results.
- 800 KL capacity is not commissioned by Q2 FY27 as the Aug 2026 presentation says.
- Borrowings rise above ~Rs 400 crore or a new equity raise is announced to fund the capacity plan.
- The devices slump sale is completed on terms that move devices outside the listed entity's control, or the Dr. Morepen stake is sold further.
- Promoter holding falls below 35% or any pledge appears.

## Questions only you can answer (scuttlebutt)
- Who is the CDMO customer and over how many years is the Rs 825 crore spread? Look at whether any filing, channel check or a peer's commentary names the molecule or customer.
- Are pharma distributors/formulators seeing API price increases from China flow through in India (the 2025 call said they had not yet)?
- Devices: are glucometer/BP monitor sell-through and strip sales holding up in retail and e-commerce, and how strong is the competition from Dr Morepen's own brands vs Accu-Chek, OneTouch?
- What do sell-side or other investors in the 2026 calls say about FY27 and FY28 margin guidance? The latest transcripts were not available to me.
- What is the true ownership plan for Morepen Medipath (devices) and for the 19.94% in Dr. Morepen, and who bought the rest?
