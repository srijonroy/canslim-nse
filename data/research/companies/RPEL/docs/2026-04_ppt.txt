End users of Silica Ramming Mass?
What is Silica Ramming Mass?
Refractory material made from quartz mineral
and binder chemicals.
What is the application of Silica Ramming Mass?
A consumable applied in the inner lining of Induction
Furnaces (IF) as a protective lining and insulator. 
Why Raghav’s product stands out?
Customized product, offering increased productivity and
energy cost savings for end users, due to higher longevity
of furnace lining.
