## The business in 5 lines
Sky Gold & Diamonds is a B2B maker of lightweight gold jewellery that sells to retail chains: Kalyan, Malabar, Joyalukkas, Senco, CaratLane, Titan Gold, Reliance Jewels and others (2026-06 transcript p.4).
Revenue grew from ₹1,154 crore in FY23 to ₹6,295 crore in FY26 (facts.md). Profit grew from ₹19 crore to ₹282 crore.
It earns a thin margin on making charges: EBITDA was 7.8% in Q1 FY27, and gold-price risk is hedged back to back (2026-08 transcript p.5, p.8).
The plan for the next phase ("Sky Gold 3.0"): grow revenue 30–35% a year, shift to "Advance Gold" (customers supply the gold, and Sky Gold books only fees), sell more studded and lab-grown diamond jewellery, export more, and be net-debt-free by FY30.
**The key fact to understand:** most of the recent revenue growth comes from the gold price. Q1 FY27 revenue was up 78%, but volume was up only 7–9% (2026-08 transcript p.12).

## Red flags
No confirmed red flag. **Checked:** the auditor's fraud reporting (none), qualified opinion (none), related-party deals (stated as arm's length), and promoter stake (fell through dilution, not sales). **Suspected, to follow up:**
1. The ₹10.7 crore incident disclosed on 16 Jul 2026. Management compares it with incidents at other listed companies, which suggests it was a fraud *against* the company; ₹3.5 crore has been recovered. Read the 16 Jul exchange filing.
2. Debt has risen every year while the stock re-rated. The debt-halving promise depends on a land sale, with promoters as the backstop buyer.
3. Withdrawing volume guidance right after volumes slowed makes revenue harder to judge.

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap about ₹12,500 crore (₹811 a share). On TTM profit of ₹343 crore that's a **P/E of about 37**; on FY27 profit implied by guidance (₹8,100 crore revenue × ~5% PAT margin ≈ ₹400 crore) about **31×**; on the FY30 target (₹945 crore) about **13×**.
The price assumes management roughly hits its profit plan. If the gold price falls back, revenue (and the market's enthusiasm) would fall even though margins are hedged.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** The client list is already the top retail chains, so growth comes from selling more to them, plus Advance Gold and studded jewellery lifting margins and ROCE (35%) while operating cash flow has turned positive. Institutions are steadily buying. If net debt really halves in FY27, falling interest costs add to profit.
- **Bear:** Real growth is roughly 8% volume, and the rest is a record gold price. If gold falls or government curbs bite, revenue growth collapses even though margins are hedged. Debt has doubled in two years, and the stock is up 155% in 6 months at 37× earnings.

## I'm wrong if
- Net debt isn't below about ₹350 crore by the Q3 FY27 results (Feb 2027), or the land sale slips with no promoter purchase.
- Operating cash flow turns negative again in any quarter of FY27.
- Net working-capital days go above 65.
- The Advance Gold share falls below 15% of volume.
- The promoter stake falls through promoters *selling* shares, as opposed to dilution.
