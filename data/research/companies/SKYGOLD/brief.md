## The business in 5 lines
Sky Gold & Diamonds is a B2B maker of lightweight gold jewellery. It sells to retail chains (Kalyan, Senco, PN Gadgil, Malabar, Joyalukkas and others) and now to Gulf and Far East retailers (2026-11 transcript p.3-5).
Revenue grew from Rs 1,154 crore in FY23 to Rs 6,292 crore in FY26 and Rs 8,083 crore TTM; net profit from Rs 19 crore to Rs 282 crore and Rs 406 crore TTM (facts.md).
Margin is thin and hedged back to back: EBITDA 7.9% in Q2 FY27, PAT 5.4% (2026-11 transcript p.11; 2026-11 ppt p.7). Gold-price risk is not taken.
Changes since the last brief: a Rs 1,800 crore QIP mandate is announced, the net-debt-halving promise is no longer made, three acquisitions (Star Mangalsutra, Rishabh Gold, Purvi Gems) are being integrated, and a Rs 10.7 crore fraud at a subsidiary is now provided for.
**Key fact:** most revenue growth is the gold price. Gold is up roughly 80% in 18 months (2026-11 transcript p.15). Volume was not disclosed on this call.

## Red flags
**Confirmed:**
1. Fraud at subsidiary Star Mangalsutra: Rs 10.70 crore, Rs 3.35 crore recovered, Rs 7.35 crore charged in Q2. Small against profit (about 6%), but it confirms that controls were weak at an acquired business (2026-11 transcript p.10-11). I treat this as the confirmed flag that caps the conviction. Check it again after the next two quarters for any further incident.

**Suspected / to follow up:**
2. The Rs 1,800 crore QIP contradicts "growth funded by internal cash". Management compares itself with Walmart's negative free cash flow years to justify it (2026-11 p.9). The stated use is not disclosed (the quiet period). It may be for acquisitions ("customers want us to buy weak makers").
3. Working-capital and cash-flow improvement leans on a Rs 345 crore jump in payables in six months. If those payables revert, operating cash flow turns negative.
4. Net-debt promise softened and land sale slipped; the fallback is a promoter purchase, a related-party deal.
5. Two quarters of withdrawn guidance (volume in March, all forward guidance in November), at the moment equity is being raised.
6. Pledged shares in the net-debt calculation (from the last brief) remain unexplained.
**Checked and clean (from the last brief, annual report not re-read):** no qualified opinion, no fraud reported by the auditors in the annual report, promoter stake falling only through dilution.

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap Rs 15,830 crore at Rs 1,022; **P/E 39.4** on TTM profit of Rs 406 crore (facts.md). H1 FY27 PAT of Rs 234 crore annualises to about Rs 470 crore, so about **34×**. On management's "more than Rs 500 crore annualised" it is about **31×**. On the FY30 target of Rs 945 crore it is about **17×**, before roughly 11% dilution from the QIP. Market cap is about 1.8× TTM sales.
The price assumes management roughly meets its FY30 profit plan (profit growing about 2.3× over four years, about 24% a year) and that the QIP money earns high returns. If gold falls, revenue falls even if margins hold.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Top retail chains are already customers, and growth comes from selling more categories to them plus exports. Advance Gold and studded jewellery lift margin and ROCE (27%). Margins hold while PAT roughly doubles. A better auditor, a professional team, and promoters taking no salary all point towards discipline. Institutions are present.
- **Bear:** Growth is mostly the gold price. Cash flow is propped up by supplier credit, and equity is being raised again after a promise not to. Guidance is now withheld, there was a fraud at an acquired unit, and the land sale and net-debt target have slipped. The stock is at 39× earnings and rising.

## I'm wrong if
- The QIP is priced or used in a way that is mostly for working capital or for large acquisitions without disclosed returns.
- Trade payables fall back below Rs 250 crore and operating cash flow in a quarter turns negative (Q3 FY27, results Feb 2027).
- Net debt is not below about Rs 400 crore at Mar-27 and the land is sold to the promoters rather than a third party.
- Net working-capital days go above 62.
- Advance Gold falls below 15% of volume, or the promoter stake falls through selling (not dilution).
- A second fraud or accounting incident appears in any subsidiary.
- Revenue growth falls below 30% in Q3 FY27 while gold is flat or falling.

## Questions only you can answer (scuttlebutt)
- What was the QIP actually for, and at what price? Read the shareholder-resolution notice and the postal ballot / AGM notice for the Rs 1,800 crore enabling mandate. Is any acquisition under way?
- Why did payables jump to Rs 437 crore? Ask a diamond supplier whether 90-120 days credit is standard for Sky Gold specifically.
- Are the Star Mangalsutra findings fully known? Read the July 2026 exchange filing for how the fraud was committed.
- Who is the land buyer, and at what price? Watch for a related-party disclosure if the promoters buy it.
- Visit a Kalyan, Malabar or Senco store. Ask whether lightweight, 18-carat and 14-carat pieces come from Sky Gold and how fast they sell against other suppliers'.
- Ask a jeweller how Sky Gold's designs and delivery compare with other B2B makers (Shanti Gold, Utssav, Rajkot and Coimbatore makers).
