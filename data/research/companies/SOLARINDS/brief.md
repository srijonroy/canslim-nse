## The business in 5 lines
- Makes commercial explosives (bulk and packaged) and initiating systems, and sells defence products (rockets, propellants, munitions). The base is Nagpur, with plants being added in North and West India and in Africa, Turkey and Asia.
- Q1 FY27 sales Rs 3,668 crore: domestic explosives 37%, international 37%, defence 26% (docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.5). FY26 sales were Rs 9,838 crore, PAT Rs 1,737 crore.
- Defence has gone from 18% of sales to 27% in FY26 (Rs 1,355 crore to Rs 2,634 crore), and to 33% in Q4 (docs/2026-05_transcript.txt p.3-4). Coal India's share of sales has fallen from 13% to 9%.
- Order book is Rs 21,350 crore (docs/2026-08_ppt.txt p.6), most of it defence (Rs 18,000 crore in Feb).
- Sep 2026: signed an all-cash deal of about $1.35 billion for Omnia Holdings (South Africa: mining explosives under the BME brand, plus an agriculture business). Not yet closed; it needs shareholder and regulatory approval.

## Red flags
No confirmed red flag. Checked: promoter holding and pledge, auditor opinion, related parties, preferential issues and warrants.

Suspected or worth watching:
- **Deal risk (suspected).** A first big overseas deal of about $1.35 billion into a segment Solar does not run (agriculture and fertiliser, "a new vertical for us"), with debt of Rs 10,000-11,000 crore planned, against Rs 1,524 crore today (docs/2026-09_transcript.txt p.5, p.7; facts.md). Management did not take any questions on the current business on that call.
- **Defence slippage (suspected).** The Rs 3,000 crore FY26 target was missed and the product milestones keep moving (Pinaka, Bhargavastra, 155 mm).
- **Inconsistent figures to check.** Q4 FY26 EBITDA is Rs 870 crore in the Q4 call and Q1 presentation, but facts.md (Screener) shows operating profit of Rs 826 crore. Probably a definition difference such as other income, but not confirmed.
- **Combined margin.** The Sep call implies about 22-23% EBITDA for the combined group, against 28% for Solar alone, because Omnia is a lower-margin business.

## Valuation (not scored)
- P/E 90.9, market cap Rs 1,80,980 crore, price Rs 20,000 (facts.md). That is about 16x trailing sales of Rs 11,351 crore and about 88x trailing profit of Rs 2,050 crore.
- On management's FY27 sales target of Rs 14,000 crore at the current net margin of about 18%, profit would be about Rs 2,500 crore. That is about 72x (my arithmetic, not a management figure).
- The Sep call implies profit before tax of Rs 5,000 crore+ in FY28 for the combined group, after about Rs 1,000-1,100 crore of interest. If that were met, the P/E on FY28 would be roughly 50x on tax-adjusted profit (my arithmetic).
- The price assumes profit roughly doubling in two years, including the Omnia deal closing and being integrated. It leaves little room for defence slippage or a weaker Omnia.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Defence is now a quarter to a third of sales, backed by an order book of Rs 21,350 crore. International is growing fast, margins have held at 27-28%, and ROCE is 38%. Omnia could make Solar a leading African explosives player, with the stated EBITDA reaching Rs 7,000 crore by FY28.
- **Bear:** Already priced at 91x earnings. Defence delivery has been late. Part of the FY27 growth is just higher prices passed through, working capital is lengthening, and the Omnia deal adds a big debt load and a new agriculture business at a low-margin mix.

## I'm wrong if
- Defence revenue for H1 FY27 is below Rs 2,000 crore (the FY27 target of Rs 4,500 crore then needs a very steep H2).
- Sales growth falls below 30% year on year in Q2 or Q3 FY27, or quarterly EBITDA margin drops below 25%.
- The cash conversion cycle goes above 160 days, or debtor days above 75.
- The Omnia deal is not approved, or financing pushes debt above 2x EBITDA.
- Promoter holding drops below 70%, or any pledge appears.
