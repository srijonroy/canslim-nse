## The business in 5 lines
India's largest integrated power company: thermal and hydro generation (17.4 GW installed), renewables (7.2 GW in-house), Mumbai and TBCB transmission, distribution to 13.3 mn customers (Mumbai, Delhi, Odisha), rooftop solar, and cell/module manufacturing (~1 GW modules a quarter). Earns mostly regulated returns plus contracted PPAs. FY26 revenue Rs 63,681 crore, EBITDA Rs 16,090 crore, PAT Rs 5,118 crore (docs/2026-11_ppt.txt). Last 2 years: debt up from Rs 53,689 crore (Mar 2024) to Rs 76,141 crore (Mar 2026) as capex ramps to a stated Rs 25,000 crore a year, Mundra back running under Section 11, solar manufacturing built, hydro/pumped-storage projects started. Revenue has been flat since FY25 (65,478 to 62,429) while operating profit rose.

## Red flags
- **Confirmed:** USD 490.32 million (about Rs 4,000+ crore) arbitration award against the company, July/August 2025. The auditor drew attention to it in both standalone and consolidated reports (docs/annual_report_2026.txt, Note 39 / Note 40). No provision made; set-aside application pending in Singapore. This is a cap on conviction.
- Suspected: capex guidance repeatedly overshoots (Rs 25,000 crore vs about Rs 13,000 crore in FY26). Not a governance issue but weakens the Rs 25,000 crore FY27 target.
- Suspected: earnings quality in the Mar 2026 quarter, other income 99% of profit before tax (facts.md).
- Tata Projects continues to lose money and losses widened in Q1 FY27 (docs/2026-07_transcript.txt).
- Checked and clear: no promoter pledge found in the annual report; no qualified audit opinion; promoter stake flat at 46.9%. Related-party transactions were not read in detail.

## Valuation (not scored)
P/E 28.4 at Rs 348 (facts.md snapshot; price Rs 345 on 30 Oct). Market cap Rs 1,11,230 crore is 1.75x TTM sales (Rs 63,445 crore). Net debt Rs 62,681 crore, so enterprise value is about Rs 1.74 lakh crore, about 13.6x TTM operating profit (Rs 12,816 crore). Management's FY30 PAT aim of Rs 10,000 crore is a P/E of about 11 on today's cap; but reaching it from Rs 5,118 crore means about 18% a year for 4 years, against 7-12% in the past 3 years. The price assumes mid-teens profit growth for 4 years, with debt funding it. ROE 10-12% is a utility's, not a growth stock's.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** RE capacity going from 7 GW toward 9.5 GW, TBCB lines starting to earn from Q2 onward, Mundra under long-term SPPA, regulated transmission growth; PAT could compound near management's Rs 10,000 crore aim.
- **Bear:** Net debt keeps climbing at Rs 25,000 crore a year capex against ROCE of 10.5%; capex and commissioning dates slip repeatedly; curtailment of about 5% hits RE returns; the USD 490 million award is unprovided; the stock fails every CAN SLIM growth test (RS 46, ROE 10%).

## I'm wrong if
- Net debt/underlying EBITDA goes above 3.6 or net debt above Rs 70,000 crore by Mar 2027.
- RE installed capacity is below 8.5 GW by Mar 2027.
- FY27 capex comes in below Rs 20,000 crore (second year of missing).
- Singapore court dismisses the set-aside application and a provision or payment of USD 490 million follows.
- FY27 PAT is below Rs 6,000 crore (the 4.5x-5x of Q1 claim).
- Promoter stake falls below 46% or a pledge appears.
