## The business in 5 lines
TD Power Systems makes **electric generators** (below 100 MW) and motors that other companies' engines and turbines drive: gas engines, gas turbines, steam turbines and hydro. Its customers are "all the major engine makers, major turbine makers" worldwide (2026-08 transcript p.6–7).
**Export-driven**: 79% of FY26 order inflow and 93% of Q1 FY27 inflow were exports. Most of it goes to the US for **behind-the-meter power at AI data centres** and for grid stabilisation (2026-05 transcript p.3; 2026-08 transcript p.2–3, p.8).
FY26 revenue ₹1,856 crore and profit ₹239 crore; Q1 FY27 sales +74% and profit +81%; order book ₹2,208 crore (2026-08 transcript p.2; facts.md).
Plans: FY27 revenue ₹2,600 crore; capacity of about ₹3,200 crore for FY28 through ₹50 crore of debottlenecking; a further expansion towards ₹4,000 crore+ for FY29–30; and a possible entry into **large generators (over 100 MW)**, which was to be announced in "August" (2026-08 transcript p.3–4).

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap about ₹24,600 crore (₹786 a share). On TTM profit of ₹275 crore that's a **P/E of about 89**. On FY27 guidance (₹2,600 crore × ~13% net margin ≈ ₹340 crore) about **72×**; on FY28 capacity (₹3,200 crore → ~₹420 crore profit) about **58×**.
The price assumes the data-centre power boom runs for years and TDPS keeps beating its guidance.

## Red flags
No confirmed red flag. **Suspected, to check:**
1. **The promoter stake fell 6.3 points in one quarter** (33.2% → 26.9%, Jun-25). It looks like a promoter sale (block deal), but the documents don't say. Check the exchange's SAST/insider filings for Apr–Jun 2025. If promoters keep selling into the rally, that's a red flag.
2. **Emphasis of matter** on the Turkey subsidiary's going-concern basis. It's small, but it shows a past overseas bet that didn't work.
**Checked and clean:** pledge (none), qualified opinion (none), dilution (none), related parties (standard omnibus approvals).

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** A global supplier to every major engine and turbine maker in a market with a real equipment shortage. Orders are growing 50%+, management under-promises and beats, it has no debt, institutions are steadily buying, and the large-generator entry could add a second leg.
- **Bear:** It depends heavily on one theme (US data-centre power) and one export market facing tariff risk. At 72× forward earnings, any order slowdown would hit the price hard. Debtor days are creeping up, and a promoter appears to have sold.

## I'm wrong if
- Quarterly order inflow falls below ₹550 crore (the Oct 2025 level) for two quarters.
- FY27 guidance is cut, or Q2–Q3 sales run below a ₹2,400 crore annual pace.
- EBITDA margin drops below 17%.
- Promoters sell more stock, or the large-generator deal is announced on terms that need heavy capex or dilution.
- US tariffs on Indian generators rise and customers stop absorbing them.
