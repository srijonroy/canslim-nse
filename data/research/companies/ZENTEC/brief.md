## The business in 5 lines
Zen Technologies (Hyderabad) makes defence training simulators (army, navy, now air) and counter-drone systems, with in-house IP and a growing set of subsidiaries (naval sim, EW, drone motors, robotics, AI). Customer is mostly the Indian MoD and armed forces; export order book is only Rs 92 crore of Rs 1,376 crore.
Sales are very lumpy: Rs 974 crore in FY25, Rs 688 crore in FY26 (-29%), TTM Rs 843 crore (facts.md). Q2 FY27 was a record Rs 345 crore (+99%), Q1 was Rs 142 crore. Management says revenue is weighted to Q2 and Q3.
Operating margin 34-38% in a normal quarter; gross margin about 73% (docs/2026-07_transcript.txt p.3). Debt free, cash Rs 968 crore at Sep 2026.
In the last 2 years: Rs 1,000 crore QIP raised, several acquisitions (naval sim, EW, drone propulsion, AI, robotics, and Cogknit for command and control), and a large post-Operation Sindoor counter-drone order of about Rs 800 crore.
The stock now prices a large step-up in orders that has been promised for over a year and has not yet arrived.

## Red flags
No confirmed red flags. Suspected or worth watching:
- Cash conversion: working-capital cycle went 196 → 255 → 379 days in two quarters; H1 operating cash flow negative; cash down Rs 249 crore in one quarter (docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.3-4, docs/2026-07_transcript.txt p.3). I did not see the cash flow statement, so how much of the cash fall is acquisitions (e.g. Cogknit) is unchecked.
- Credibility of order timing: the simulator order pipeline estimate fell from Rs 700-800 crore (Jul) to about Rs 400 crore (Nov), and the Rs 4,000 crore target is now hedged.
- Q2 PAT growth (+50.7% on +98.8% sales) shows costs rising: employee and other expenses up (warranty provision Rs 9.3 crore higher, R&D headcount) (docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.2-3).
- Acquisition spree into adjacent areas (Cogknit "at least five years from now" for revenue) — an analyst questioned the fit (docs/2026-11_transcript.txt p.6-7).
- Checked and clean: auditor opinion, pledge, warrants/convertibles, preferential issues (docs/annual_report_2026.txt). Not checked: related-party values.

## Valuation (not scored)
Market cap Rs 15,744 crore; P/E 74.1 (facts.md); about 19x TTM sales of Rs 843 crore and 8x book value (Rs 220/share vs price 1,744).
Management does not give a FY27 profit number. If FY27 sales are roughly Rs 1,100-1,300 crore (the Rs 1,000 crore of equipment orders due in FY27 plus AMC and new orders, my estimate) at the 25% PAT margin target, profit is about Rs 275-325 crore, so P/E about 50-57x on FY27. If the Rs 4,000 crore two-year target is met (say Rs 1,300 crore in FY27, Rs 2,700 crore in FY28), FY28 PAT at 25% is about Rs 675 crore, about 23x. In plain words: the price assumes the Rs 4,000 crore target is mostly met, which needs order book to roughly double by March 2027. If FY28 sales stay near Rs 1,500 crore, the P/E on FY28 is about 42x with no margin of safety.

## Bull case / bear case
- **Bull:** Government counter-drone FTP tenders and simulator orders land in Q4 FY27; order book reaches about Rs 2,500 crore as guided; owned IP and 35%+ margins scale on a fixed cost base and the debt-free balance sheet funds it all.
- **Bear:** Orders keep slipping (they already did), revenue stays lumpy, working capital keeps absorbing the Rs 968 crore cash, R&D and acquisitions push costs up, and a P/E of 74 on a cyclical-looking earnings base de-rates.

## I'm wrong if
- Order book at Mar 2027 is below Rs 2,000 crore (guided about Rs 2,500 crore).
- Q3 FY27 sales are below Rs 250 crore, or the FY27 operating margin is below 33%.
- No C-UAS FTP tender awarded to Zen by Jun 2027.
- Working-capital cycle is still above 300 days at Mar 2027 or cash drops below Rs 700 crore with no matching receivables collection.
- Promoter stake falls below 45%, or any pledge appears.
