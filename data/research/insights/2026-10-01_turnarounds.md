takeaway: A list without CAN SLIM's ROE and 3-year growth rules (catching turnarounds like Laurus 2019–21) made 21.9%/yr in 2016–22 but fell up to 39%, vs 23.8% and −21% for CAN SLIM. Kept as a separate, higher-risk list.

**Why.** CAN SLIM missed or caught late several big turnarounds (Laurus 2019–21, Wockhardt 2024, Avanti 2016–17) because of its ROE, 3-year growth and liquidity rules.

**Emerging / turnaround list.** The same earnings signals (margin jump or two strong quarters), RS ≥ 80, uptrend and near the high, with no ROE or 3-year rule, from ₹5 Cr/day traded.

| 2016–22 | Return/yr | Worst drop |
|---|---|---|
| CAN SLIM | 23.8% | −21% |
| Emerging list | 21.9% | −38.6% |
| Both combined | worse than CAN SLIM alone | |

**Lesson.** Quality rules buy a smoother ride. Turnarounds are worth watching separately, sized smaller.
