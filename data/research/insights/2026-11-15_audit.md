**Question.** Every result on the Insights page comes from one backtester and one price/fundamentals pipeline. Do the dates, prices, split adjustments and portfolio arithmetic hold up against independent sources?

**Method.** Five checks against sources the backtest does not use: NSE result filing timestamps, a separately written backtester, NSE bhavcopy prices, NSE corporate actions, and the NSE index archive.

### 1. Result dates
59678 stock-quarters since Sep 2015 matched to an NSE result filing (76% of all). A filing counts as public for the decision at a day's close if NSE received it before the next 09:15 open. Our date vs NSE: ours later by 8+ days 17.9%, ours later 1-7 days 12.4%, same day 59.9%, ours earlier 1 day 1.1%, ours earlier 2+ days 8.7%.

Backtest with every result date set to the later of ours and NSE's: 2016-22 25.6% → 25.6% a year (Sharpe 1.34 → 1.34, worst drop -21.1 → -21.1); 2023+ 34.4% → 34.4% (Sharpe 1.41 → 1.41).

No trade was bought before its result was filed.

### 2. Separately written backtester
`plain_backtest` in this file was written from the rules in plain English, without reusing `canslim.portfolio`: same ranked pool, 10 slots, keep while rank ≤ 30, no new buys outside an uptrend, max 3 per industry, next-open fills, 0.3% a side, cash at 6%.

| period | canslim.portfolio | rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| 2016-22 | 25.6% / Sharpe 1.34 / DD -21.1 | 25.6% / 1.34 / -21.1 |
| 2023+ | 34.4% / Sharpe 1.41 / DD -14.2 | 34.4% / 1.41 / -14.2 |

### 4. Split and bonus adjustments
